## 01 / The person behind the science

I work at the intersection of **chemistry, scientific reasoning, and AI content quality**. My experience spans reviewing expert-authored chemistry questions, verifying solutions, and helping subject matter experts produce clear, scientifically sound content for AI training and assessment.

I am pursuing a **Ph.D. in Chemistry at Lovely Professional University**, with a master's degree in Organic Chemistry and experience in both university labs and online chemistry education.

My approach is simple: check the science, examine every reasoning step, and make sure the question contains everything needed to solve it.
